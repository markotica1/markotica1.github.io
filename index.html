How I lead

Three principles.
Every program.

01

Design the system before scaling the team.

Headcount is the most expensive fix. I start with the operating model — workflows, priorities, controls — which is how deflection grew 700%+ with a flat team.

02

Turn safety goals into roadmaps people can execute.

Working across Legal, Compliance, and outsourcing partners, I translate risk and policy intent into measurable plans, milestones, and owners.

03

Build leaders, not just processes.

From 12 Team Leads to ~200 Lean Six Sigma graduates, the goal is a team that finds and fixes problems without waiting to be told.
